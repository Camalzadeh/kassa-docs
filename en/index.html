Cashier quick guide
Installation
Download PDF

🧾 Selling

Scan the barcode — the product goes into the cart. Cash (F2) or card (F3); the receipt prints by itself.

📦 Products and labels

New products, barcodes for products without one, label printing.

🚚 Receiving goods

Scan incoming goods, enter the quantity and cost price — stock goes up by itself.

📊 Stock and reports

What is left, what was sold, profit, products running low.

🖨️ Printer

Receipt and label printer (e.g. Xprinter) — direct printing, no print dialog.

📱 Telegram

A report and an encrypted backup on your phone every evening.

💾 Backup and restore

The program backs up by itself. Even if the computer breaks, the data is not lost.
