Products, barcodes and labels ​

Admin only.

New product ​

Products → New product:

FieldWhat to enter
BarcodeScan the product's barcode
NameThe name the cashier and the receipt will show
UnitPiece or Kilogram (by weight)
Sale priceThe price the customer pays
Cost priceThe price you paid (for profit calculation; the cashier does not see it)
Minimum stockBelow this, the "Low" warning appears

If a barcode that is not in the system is scanned during a sale or receiving, this window opens by itself.

Products without a barcode ​

Bread, herbs, your own products: the Generate barcode button → the program gives an in-store barcode (starts with 29). Then print its label and stick it on the product.

Changing the price ​

✏️ on the product's line → new price → Save. The change is written to the log. To stop selling a product: untick Active (the product is not deleted, its history stays).

Printing labels ​

  1. Products → tick the products you need (or 🏷 on a single product's line).
  2. Print labels → type:
    • A4 sheet — 24 labels (70×37 mm) or 40 labels;
    • label printer roll — 58×40, 50×30, 40×30 or 30×20 mm (the size of the label on the roll).
  3. How many of each product: +.
  4. Print. If a label printer is selected, the button shows the printer's name — labels print without a print dialog.

With the print dialog (no printer selected), the first time

Choose Scale: 100% (Actual size) and Margins: None. Do a test print on plain paper first.

After the label prints, check its barcode with the scanner — you should hear a "beep".
